Frequently asked questions

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, select OrbitBloom, and choose to manage or cancel. You can also tap "Manage Subscription" inside the App's Settings screen.
How does the free trial work?
The yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers. You won't be charged until the trial ends. You can cancel any time before the trial ends to avoid being charged. The monthly plan does not include a trial.
What's included in the free tier?
You can add and organise up to 25 active connections for free, along with stages, notes, reminders, and trips. Premium unlocks unlimited connections, AI insights, unlimited photo capture, and personalised suggestions. Premium "unlimited" features are intended for normal personal use and may be subject to reasonable fair-use limits to prevent abuse.
Is my data private?
Yes. Your notes and personal details stay on your device. Photos are never stored on our servers. When AI features are used, only lightweight context and first names are processed — never your private notes. Your data is never sold or used to train AI models. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.
How do I delete my account and data?
Open the App's Settings screen and tap "Delete account." This permanently deletes your account and associated data. You can also contact us at the email above if you need help.
I restored my purchase but Premium isn't active.
In the App, open the paywall and tap "Restore Purchases." Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID used for the original purchase. If it still doesn't activate, email us and we'll help.
How do I report a bug or request a feature?
We'd love to hear from you. Email support@kloud9ai.com.au with as much detail as you can, including your device and what happened.
